# FlowDesk Support

FlowDesk Support can help customers with account access, subscriptions, billing, payments, cancellations, and refunds.

For billing-related requests, customers should provide their account email address and relevant subscription information.

Refund requests should include the purchase date, subscription type, and reason for the request.

Support agents may request additional information when a billing transaction needs to be verified.

General support requests are normally handled through the FlowDesk Support portal.

Enterprise customers should contact their assigned account representative when their question concerns contractual billing, cancellation, renewal, or refund terms.
