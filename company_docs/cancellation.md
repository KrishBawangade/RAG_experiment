# FlowDesk Subscription Cancellation

Customers can cancel their FlowDesk subscription at any time from the Billing section of their account.

Cancelling a subscription stops the next automatic renewal.

For annual subscriptions, cancellation does not normally terminate access immediately. The customer continues to have access until the end of the current annual billing period.

For monthly subscriptions, cancellation keeps the account active until the end of the current monthly billing period.

Cancellation and refunds are separate processes. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically create a refund request.

Customers who want both cancellation and a refund must cancel the subscription and separately submit a refund request if they believe they meet the refund eligibility requirements.

After cancellation, customers can continue using FlowDesk until their paid subscription period expires.
