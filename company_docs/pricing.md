# FlowDesk Pricing

FlowDesk offers three standard subscription plans: Starter, Professional, and Business.

The Starter plan costs $10 per month or $100 per year.

The Professional plan costs $25 per month or $250 per year.

The Business plan costs $60 per month or $600 per year.

Annual subscriptions provide a lower effective monthly price compared with paying month-to-month.

Customers can change plans from the Billing section of their FlowDesk account.

Plan pricing may change for future subscription periods. Existing customers are generally notified before a pricing change affects a renewal.

Discounts and promotional pricing may have separate eligibility and expiration conditions.
