# FlowDesk Enterprise Plans

FlowDesk Enterprise plans are designed for organizations with large teams and customized requirements.

Enterprise customers may receive custom pricing, dedicated support, advanced security controls, and customized contractual terms.

Enterprise subscriptions may use annual contracts rather than the standard monthly or annual subscription plans available through the FlowDesk website.

Refund, cancellation, renewal, and payment terms for Enterprise customers are determined by the customer's contract.

Enterprise customers should refer to their signed agreement for specific billing and cancellation requirements.

For questions about Enterprise billing, customers can contact their assigned FlowDesk account representative or Enterprise Support.
