# FlowDesk Subscriptions

FlowDesk offers monthly and annual subscription plans for individual and business customers.

Monthly subscriptions renew every month on the date the subscription was originally created. Annual subscriptions renew once every twelve months from the original purchase date.

Customers can upgrade from a monthly subscription to an annual subscription at any time. When upgrading, the remaining value of the current monthly billing period may be applied toward the new annual subscription.

Annual subscriptions remain active until their expiration date unless the customer cancels them before the next renewal.

Cancelling an annual subscription prevents the next annual renewal but does not immediately terminate access. Customers normally retain access until the end of their current billing period.

Subscription changes can be managed from the Billing section of the FlowDesk account dashboard.
