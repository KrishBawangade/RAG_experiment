# FlowDesk Accounts

Every FlowDesk subscription is associated with a customer account.

Customers can manage their profile, billing information, subscription settings, and payment methods from the account dashboard.

The account email address is used for billing notifications, subscription renewal reminders, and support communication.

Customers should keep their account information accurate so that billing and subscription notifications are delivered correctly.

Changing an account email address does not change the subscription plan or billing period.

If a customer loses access to their account, they should contact FlowDesk Support and provide information that can be used to verify account ownership.
