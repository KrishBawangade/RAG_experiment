# FlowDesk Payments

FlowDesk accepts major credit cards and supported debit cards for subscription payments.

Subscription payments are processed automatically at the beginning of each billing period.

If an automatic payment fails, FlowDesk may retry the payment using the customer's saved payment method.

Customers can update their payment method from the Billing section of their account.

A failed payment does not immediately cancel an active subscription. Customers normally receive notifications and have an opportunity to update their payment information.

If a payment remains unsuccessful after the applicable retry period, the subscription may be suspended.

Payment processing problems and refund requests are handled as separate billing issues.
