# FlowDesk Refund Policy

FlowDesk provides refunds for eligible subscription purchases.

Customers who purchase an annual subscription may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.

Refund requests submitted after the 30-day period are normally not eligible for a refund unless an exception is approved by the FlowDesk billing team.

Monthly subscription payments are generally non-refundable once the monthly billing period has started.

A refund applies to the eligible subscription payment. Refunds do not automatically restore or extend a cancelled subscription.

If a customer has upgraded from a monthly plan to an annual plan, the billing team may review the transaction history before determining the refundable amount.

Refund requests can be submitted through FlowDesk Support with the account email address, subscription type, purchase date, and reason for the request.
